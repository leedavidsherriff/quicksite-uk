Plenty of bathroom and kitchen problems don't need a full plumbing call-out: a dripping tap, a toilet that won't stop running, a wobbly seat, blackened silicone or a cracked bath panel. We sort small plumbing and bathroom fixes for homes and rentals in Trowbridge, usually on the same visit as other jobs on your list. We'll always tell you straight away if something is beyond a minor repair, such as a hidden leak, a boiler fault or anything to do with gas, and point you towards the right specialist rather than trying to bodge it.

Dripping taps and running toilets

Most dripping taps are caused by a worn washer in older taps, or a worn ceramic cartridge in modern quarter-turn and mixer taps. The water is isolated, the tap is taken apart and the worn part replaced, and it's worth taking the old part to match the size. A toilet that keeps running or refilling usually has a faulty fill valve or a worn flush valve seal, both of which are standard replacements. Isolation valves under sinks and toilets make these jobs quicker; if you don't have them, we can fit them so future repairs are easier. Before we start, we'll show you where your main stopcock is, which is worth knowing in any emergency.

Silicone, seats and bath panels

Black, peeling silicone around baths, showers and sinks is one of the quickest ways to make a bathroom look tired, and failed silicone can let water through into floors and ceilings. Old silicone is cut out completely, the joint cleaned and treated for mould, then fresh sanitary silicone is applied; baths are filled with water first so the joint doesn't split. Toilet seats are a simple swap, but it helps to measure the pan and fixing holes before buying. Cracked or discoloured bath panels can be replaced, and we'll make sure the panel is supported by a proper frame. Loose basins and toilet pans can be refixed to the wall or floor with new fixings so they stop moving.

Knowing where the limits are

We're clear about what's minor and what isn't. Anything involving gas, including boilers, gas fires and hobs, must only be done by a registered gas engineer. Connecting new electrical circuits, or electrical work in bathrooms, needs a registered electrician. Hidden leaks, low water pressure, noisy pipes, new bathroom suites and anything that means moving pipework are jobs for a plumber. If we find a problem that's bigger than expected, we'll stop, explain what we've found and help you get the right person in.

Questions about minor plumbing and bathroom fixes

Can a handyperson fix a leaking tap?

Yes, most dripping taps are a straightforward repair. Older taps usually need a new washer, and modern taps often need a new ceramic cartridge. We isolate the water, replace the worn part and test for leaks. If the tap body is corroded or damaged, replacing the whole tap may be better value.

Do you work on boilers or gas appliances?

No. By law, work on gas appliances, including boilers, gas fires and hobs, must only be carried out by a registered gas engineer. If you have a boiler problem or smell gas, contact a registered engineer, or the gas emergency line if you suspect a leak.

How often should bathroom silicone be replaced?

It depends on use and ventilation, but many bathrooms need resealing every few years. Replace it sooner if it's peeling, has black mould inside it, or water is getting behind it. Good ventilation and wiping down after showering helps silicone last longer.
