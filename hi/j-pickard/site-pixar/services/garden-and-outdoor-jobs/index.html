For larger landscaping, roofing or brickwork, we'll tell you honestly when it's a job for a specialist.

Fence panels, posts and gates

Most fence panels in the UK are 6 ft wide and slot between timber or concrete posts. If the posts are sound, replacing a blown or rotten panel is quick. If a timber post has rotted at ground level, which is common, it will need replacing, often with a concrete or treated timber post set in postcrete. Concrete posts with gravel boards last much longer. Sagging gates usually need their hinges tightened or replaced, a diagonal brace added, or the post straightened. Latches are realigned so the gate closes and latches cleanly. Before digging for new posts, we check for buried cables and pipes, particularly near house walls and outside lights.

Gutters and drainage

Gutters blocked with leaves, moss and silt overflow, which can soak walls and cause damp inside. They should be cleared at least once a year, ideally after autumn leaf fall. We clear gutters on single and two-storey houses where they can be reached safely from ladders, flush the downpipes and check for leaking joints, which can often be resealed. Gutters that have sagged or cracked can be refixed or have sections replaced. For three-storey houses, or gutters that can't be reached safely with ladders, a specialist with access equipment is safer.

Sheds, pressure washing and small builds

Sheds and garden storage need a firm, level base, such as paving slabs, a concrete pad or a timber frame on bearers, or the doors won't close properly and the floor will rot. We can prepare simple bases and assemble sheds, storage boxes and summerhouses, and felt shed roofs. Pressure washing patios, paths and driveways removes algae and grime, but it can strip sand from joints, so we recommend re-sanding block paving afterwards. Softer surfaces, like sandstone, need lower pressure to avoid damage. We'll also check that the new shed sits clear of fences and boundaries, and that its doors can open fully without hitting paths or plants.

Questions about garden and outdoor jobs

Who is responsible for a shared fence?

It's often shown on your property deeds, sometimes marked with a T on the boundary line. If it's not clear, it's worth talking to your neighbour before replacing a shared fence. The face of the panel and posts usually goes towards your neighbour, but this is a custom rather than a legal rule.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

At least once a year for most houses, usually in late autumn after the leaves have fallen. Houses near trees may need clearing twice a year. Signs that gutters need clearing include water overflowing during rain, plants growing in the gutters and damp patches on walls below.

Can you build a shed base?

Yes, for simple bases such as paving slabs or a timber frame on bearers. The base must be level and slightly larger than the shed. For a concrete base or large summerhouse, a landscaper may be better. We'll advise on the right base for your shed size and garden.
