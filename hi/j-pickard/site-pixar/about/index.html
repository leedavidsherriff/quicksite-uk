About

About J Pickard Home Improvements

J Pickard Home Improvements is a handyperson service based in Trowbridge, taking care of the small jobs around the home that builders are too busy for and that never seem to get done. That means flat-pack built properly and fixed to the wall, TVs and shelves put up with the right fixings, doors that stick eased and rehung, small painting jobs, dripping taps, fresh silicone, fence panels and gutters. Every job is priced before we start, so there are no surprises, and a list of jobs can be done in one visit to keep costs down. We'll always be honest when a job needs a specialist, such as a registered gas engineer or an electrician. We bring the tools, protect your home, tidy up and take away packaging if you'd like. Whether it's one small job or a list, call 07479 951577 and we'll book you in.

How it works

  1. Send your list

    Tell us the jobs, ideally with photos and sizes, and we'll price each one.

  2. Book a visit

    We agree a time and let you know what to have ready, such as items to be fitted.

  3. Get it done

    We bring the tools and fixings, work through the list and check each job with you.

  4. Tidy and pay

    We clean up, take away packaging if agreed, and you pay on completion.

What we promise

  • Every job priced before we start
  • The right fixings for your walls, every time
  • Honest advice when a job needs a specialist
  • Your home left clean and tidy
  • We'll come back to fix anything that isn't right
