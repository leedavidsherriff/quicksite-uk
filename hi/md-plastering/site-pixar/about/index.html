About MD Plastering

MD Plastering is a plastering business based in Hengoed, working on homes, rentals and small commercial properties across the area. Most of what we do is making walls and ceilings flat and smooth again: skimming over tired surfaces, replastering where the old plaster has failed, covering artex and putting up new ceilings. We also render outside walls and work with lime on older buildings that need to breathe. We'll always tell you honestly whether a wall needs a quick skim or something more, and we'll point out damp or movement rather than cover it up. We protect your home while we work, bag up waste as we go and leave every room clean, with clear advice on drying and decorating. If you've got a wall, ceiling or house that needs attention, call 07469 237714 and we'll arrange a visit to take a look.

How it works

  1. Survey and quote

    We tap-test the walls, check for damp and movement, and send a written quote with prep, plastering and waste itemised.

  2. Protect the room

    Floors are sheeted, doorways sealed, sockets loosened and furniture covered or moved out.

  3. Prepare

    Hollow areas are cut out, surfaces bonded or primed, beads fitted and cracks taped.

  4. Plaster

    Backing coats, boarding or skim are applied and trowelled to a flat, smooth finish.

  5. Clean up and aftercare

    Waste is removed, the room is cleaned and we explain drying times and the right first coat of paint.

What we promise

  • A written, itemised quote before we start
  • Honest advice on whether you need a skim or a replaster
  • Floors and fittings protected throughout
  • Waste removed and the room left clean
  • We'll come back to fix any defect in our work
