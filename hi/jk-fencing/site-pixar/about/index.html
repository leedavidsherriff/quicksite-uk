About JK Fencing & Landscaping

JK Fencing & Landscaping is a small landscaping team working across Swindon and the surrounding area. The person who visits your garden and prices the job is part of the team that builds it, so what we agree is what gets done, and you always have a direct number to call. We believe good landscaping is mostly the parts you never see: a compacted base under the patio, deep footings for walls and fence posts, drainage behind anything holding back soil, and ground properly prepared before turf goes down. We build patios, lawns, artificial grass, fencing, decking, raised beds and walls, and we give honest advice on materials, upkeep and cost. We protect your home while we work, tidy up every day and take the waste away. Call 07912 690100 to book a free garden visit.

How it works

  1. Garden visit

    We walk the garden with you, measure up, check levels and drainage, and talk through how you want to use the space.

  2. Design and written quote

    You get a simple plan and a fixed written price, with materials, groundwork and waste removal listed clearly.

  3. Groundwork

    We clear the area, dig out, set levels and build the bases, footings and drainage everything else depends on.

  4. Build and finish

    Patios, lawns, fences, decks and walls go in, then the garden is tidied, swept and cleared of waste.

  5. Handover and aftercare

    We walk round with you, explain any aftercare for turf, timber or planting, and confirm your guarantee in writing.

What we promise

  • A fixed written price before work starts
  • Groundwork, bases and drainage written into the quote
  • Public liability insurance, with the certificate shown on request
  • The garden tidied every day and cleared at the end
  • Clear written aftercare for lawns, timber and planting
