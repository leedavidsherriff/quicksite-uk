About The Female Plumber NE

The Female Plumber NE is a local plumbing business serving Hartlepool and the surrounding area. We look after homeowners and landlords with everything from dripping taps and running toilets to burst pipes, new showers and complete bathroom refits. We keep things simple: we turn up when we say we will, explain what is wrong in plain English, and give you a written price before any planned work starts. We carry common parts on the van so most repairs are done in one visit, and we protect your home and clean up before we leave. No job is too small, and we would rather fix a tap properly than sell you a new one you do not need. If you have a leak, a plumbing problem or a bathroom plan, call The Female Plumber NE on 07359 671340 and we will help you sort it out.

How it works

  1. Call or message

    Tell us what is going on, send a photo if you can, and we will give you a guide price or book a visit.

  2. Survey and written quote

    For planned work we visit, test your pressure and pipework, and send a written quote with a clear breakdown.

  3. Agree a date

    We book the work in, order the materials and confirm what we need from you, such as clearing the room.

  4. The work

    We protect your home, do the job, test everything under pressure and tidy up as we go.

  5. Handover

    We show you the valves and stopcocks, hand over guarantees and manuals, and settle the final invoice.

What we promise

  • A written price before any planned work starts
  • Our call-out rate given on the phone before we set off
  • Everything pressure-tested before we leave
  • Your home protected and left tidy
  • We come back if anything we fitted goes wrong
