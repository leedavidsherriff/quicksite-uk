    Questions

    Things people ask me.

    More on the questions page.

    Do you charge for quotes?

    Quotes for planned work such as bathrooms, showers and pipework upgrades are free and written down, with labour, materials and any extras listed separately. For emergencies and small repairs we give you our call-out rate on the phone before we set off, so you know the cost before we arrive.

    Are you insured?

    Any plumber working in your home should carry public liability insurance. Ask us for our insurance details before work starts and we will show you the certificate. It is a sensible question to ask any trade, and a good firm will not mind.

    Do you do gas work?

    Plumbing and gas are separate skills with separate legal requirements. Any work on gas appliances or gas pipework must be done by a registered gas engineer, and it is illegal for anyone else to do it. If your job involves gas, ask to see the engineer's gas engineer ID card, which lists the types of work they are qualified for.

    How do you take payment?

    Small jobs are paid on completion by bank transfer or card. Larger jobs such as bathroom refits usually have a deposit to cover materials and a final payment when the work is finished and you are happy. The payment schedule is written on your quote, so there are no surprises.

    What guarantee do I get?

    Our workmanship is guaranteed, and the length of that guarantee is written on your quote. Products carry their own manufacturer's guarantees, and we will pass on the paperwork and registration details. If something we fitted fails, call us and we will come back.

    How messy will it be?

    We use dust sheets and floor protection, keep the work area tidy as we go, and take away old fittings and packaging. Bathroom strip-outs are dusty, so we seal doorways where we can. We leave the room clean enough to use, not a building site.
