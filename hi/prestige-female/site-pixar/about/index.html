About Prestige Female Painter & Decorator

Prestige Female Painter & Decorator is a painting and decorating business based in Selston, working on homes and small commercial properties across the local area. We believe a good paint job is mostly preparation: filling cracks properly, sealing stains, keying surfaces and cutting in by hand, so the finish looks right on the day and still looks right years later. We look after your home as if it were our own, protecting furniture and floors and leaving every room tidy at the end of each day. Quotes are written, itemised and honest, and we'll tell you if a wall needs more than paint. Whether it's one tired bedroom, a hallway that takes a battering, a kitchen that needs a new look or the outside of the house before winter, we're happy to come round, take a look and talk it through. Call 07856 753906 to arrange a visit.

How it works

  1. Free visit and quote

    We look at the rooms, check the condition of walls and woodwork, and send a written quote priced area by area.

  2. Colours and paint

    We help you choose colours and sheens, order the paint at trade rates, and agree a start date and working hours.

  3. Protect and prepare

    Furniture is moved and covered, floors sheeted, then everything is filled, sanded, primed and made sound.

  4. Paint

    Ceilings first, then walls, then woodwork, all cut in by hand and finished in full coats.

  5. Walk-round and tidy

    We clean up, put the room back and walk round with you to touch in anything before we leave.

What we promise

  • A written, itemised quote before any work starts
  • Furniture, floors and fittings protected throughout
  • Proper preparation, not paint over problems
  • A clean, tidy home at the end of every day
  • We come back and fix anything that isn't right
