Empty properties repainted fast and ready to let

Whole-property repaints between tenancies or before you move in.

An empty property is the best time to paint, and every week it sits empty costs money. We repaint flats and houses in Aberdare between tenancies, after a sale completes and before new owners move in. Landlords and agents get a clear, itemised quote, keys can be collected from the agent, and photos are sent when the job is done. For new homeowners, painting before the furniture arrives is quicker, cleaner and cheaper than working around a lived-in home, so it's worth booking as soon as you have a completion date.

Choosing a durable, easy-to-touch-up finish

In a rental, the aim is a finish that looks fresh, stands up to wear and can be patched between tenancies without repainting the whole wall. We recommend a durable matt or soft sheen in a consistent neutral across the property, recorded by brand and colour code so any future touch-up matches exactly. Kitchens and bathrooms get moisture-resistant paint to reduce mould complaints. Woodwork in a water-based satin stays white and dries fast, so a whole flat can be completed without the lingering smell of oil gloss that puts off viewers. Ceilings are normally done in a flat white, which hides minor unevenness and is quick to refresh next time.

Dealing with common end-of-tenancy damage

Typical repairs include filling holes from picture hooks, shelves and TV brackets, patching scuffs on hallways and stair walls, and sealing marks from Blu Tack, pens and smoke. Mould on bathroom ceilings is treated with a fungicidal wash before painting, and we'll flag it to you if it points to an extractor or ventilation problem. Where there is damage beyond normal wear we can photograph it before we start, which helps when agreeing any deduction from a deposit. Small repairs such as a loose door handle or a stiff hinge can usually be done at the same visit.

Turnaround and working with agents

A one or two-bedroom flat is often completed in two to three days, and a three-bedroom house in four to five, depending on condition and whether ceilings and woodwork are included. We can work to a fixed date for a new tenant or a removals van. Access can be arranged through the agent, and we leave the property locked, tidy and free of our materials, with the leftover paint labelled in a cupboard. You receive before and after photographs and an invoice itemised by room, which keeps records simple for landlords. If the property also needs cleaning, carpets or minor repairs, we can work to a sequence with other trades so painting is finished first and nothing gets marked afterwards.

Questions about rental and move-in repaints

How often should a rental property be repainted?

There's no fixed rule. Many landlords do a full repaint every few tenancies and touch-ups in between. High-traffic areas such as hallways and stairs usually need attention first. Using one recorded neutral colour across the property means small areas can be patched without it showing, which saves money over the long run.

Can you collect keys from my letting agent?

Yes. We regularly arrange access through agents and can return keys the same way. We'll confirm the start and finish dates with the agent, send you photos when the work is complete and leave the property locked and tidy, so you don't need to visit in person.

What colour should I paint a rental?

A warm neutral such as a soft white, off-white or pale grey is safest. It suits most furniture, makes rooms look larger in listing photos and is easy to touch up. Very bright white can look clinical and shows marks. We'll help you choose one colour and sheen and record it for future work.
