Wardrobes built into the room, not stood in it

Made-to-measure wardrobes that use every centimetre, from floor to ceiling.

Get my guide priceCall 07538 540293

Freestanding wardrobes waste space: gaps at the sides, dust on top and a room that never looks finished. A fitted wardrobe is built to the exact shape of your wall, running floor to ceiling and scribed to walls that are never quite straight. We design and build fitted wardrobes for bedrooms, lofts and eaves across Pontypridd, in painted MDF, veneers and solid timber, with interiors planned around what you actually own. You'll get a drawing and a clear quote before we start, so you can see exactly what's being built.

Designing the inside first

The best fitted wardrobes are designed from the inside out. We start with what you need to store: long hanging for coats and dresses, double hanging rails for shirts and trousers, shelves for jumpers, drawers for smaller items and space for shoes and suitcases. A typical double hanging section needs about a metre of width, and wardrobes need a depth of around 60 cm for hangers to sit without catching the doors. Internal drawers, pull-out trouser rails, shoe racks and LED lighting can all be included. Upper sections above the main hanging space are ideal for bedding and luggage.

Materials, doors and finishes

Most fitted wardrobes are made from MDF, which is stable, smooth and takes paint beautifully, with moisture-resistant MDF used on external walls. Carcasses can be made from melamine-faced board for durable interiors. Doors can be shaker, slab, panelled or fluted, painted in any colour or finished in oak or walnut veneer. Hinged doors give full access to the interior, while sliding doors suit tight rooms where there's no space for doors to swing. We can spray-finish doors for a factory-smooth look, or hand-paint on site. Handles, push-to-open catches and soft-close hinges are chosen to suit the style.

Awkward spaces: eaves, alcoves and sloping ceilings

Fitted furniture really comes into its own in rooms that standard wardrobes can't use. Under sloping loft ceilings, wardrobes can be built with angled tops and low-level drawers into the eaves. In alcoves beside chimney breasts, wardrobes can be recessed to sit flush with the breast, giving a clean, built-in look. Around windows and doors, units can wrap around to create window seats and storage. Walls and floors in older houses are rarely straight, so we scribe the edges of panels to fit tightly, and fit filler panels and cornices so there are no gaps.

From survey to fitting

We start with a visit to measure the space, check walls for pipes and cables, and talk through what you want to store and the look you like. You'll get a drawing and a written quote. Most of the cutting and building is done in a workshop, then installed on site in one to three days, which keeps mess and disruption in your home to a minimum. Painting can be done by us or left ready for your decorator. We'll finish by adjusting every door and drawer so everything lines up and closes smoothly.

How long it takes

Typically 2–4 weeks to make, 1–3 days to fit

Questions about fitted wardrobes

How much do fitted wardrobes cost?

Most painted MDF fitted wardrobes are priced per linear metre of wall, including doors, carcasses and a standard interior. Veneers, extra drawers, lighting and specialist hardware add to the cost. A typical double bedroom wall of two to three metres usually falls in the low thousands. We'll give you a written quote based on your design.

Do fitted wardrobes add value to a house?

They can make a house more appealing to buyers, particularly in smaller bedrooms and loft rooms where freestanding furniture doesn't fit well. Well-made, neutral fitted storage is often seen as a positive in viewings. It's best to choose a timeless style and colour if you're thinking of selling in the next few years.

Can fitted wardrobes be painted later?

Yes. Painted MDF wardrobes can be repainted at any time, like any painted woodwork. The surface just needs a light sand, a primer if changing the type of paint, and two coats. Veneered wardrobes can be refinished, but painting over real wood veneer is less easily reversed.
