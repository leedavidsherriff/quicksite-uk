About Chippie Em Building & Carpentry

Chippie Em Building & Carpentry is a carpentry and joinery business based in Pontypridd, making fitted wardrobes and alcove units, hanging doors, fitting floors, renovating staircases and doing the second fix work that finishes a room. We believe the details make the difference: panels scribed tightly to walls that are never straight, doors that close with a soft click, and joints you can't see once they're painted. Fitted furniture is designed around what you actually need to store and built to measure, mostly in the workshop, so there's less mess in your home. We'll always give you a written quote, and a drawing for anything we make, before any work starts. We protect your floors, clear up every day and leave the room ready to use. Whether it's one door or a house full of storage, call 07538 540293 and we'll arrange a visit to measure up.

How it works

  1. Visit and measure

    We measure the space, check walls and floors, and talk through what you want to store, fit or fix.

  2. Design and quote

    You get a written quote and, for fitted furniture, a drawing showing sizes, doors and interior layout.

  3. Make

    Units and doors are built and, if chosen, spray-finished in the workshop.

  4. Fit

    Everything is installed, scribed to walls and floors, and doors and drawers are adjusted.

  5. Finish and tidy

    Joints are filled and caulked, the room is cleaned and all offcuts are taken away.

What we promise

  • A written quote, with drawings for fitted furniture
  • Units scribed to fit your walls, not gaps filled with sealant
  • Doors and drawers adjusted to close properly
  • A clean, tidy home at the end of every day
  • We'll come back to adjust or fix anything that isn't right
