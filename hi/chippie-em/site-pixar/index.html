    How it works

    From hello to handover.

    No hard sell. You already have a guide price, the survey fixes it in writing.

    1. Visit and measure

      We measure the space, check walls and floors, and talk through what you want to store, fit or fix.

    2. Design and quote

      You get a written quote and, for fitted furniture, a drawing showing sizes, doors and interior layout.

    3. Make

      Units and doors are built and, if chosen, spray-finished in the workshop.

    4. Fit

      Everything is installed, scribed to walls and floors, and doors and drawers are adjusted.

    5. Finish and tidy

      Joints are filled and caulked, the room is cleaned and all offcuts are taken away.

    Questions

    Things people ask me.

    More on the questions page.

    Do you give free quotes?

    Yes. We visit, measure and talk through what you want, then send a written quote. For fitted furniture you'll also get a drawing before anything is made.

    Do you supply materials?

    Yes, we can supply everything, from doors and flooring to MDF, timber and hardware. If you've chosen your own doors or flooring, we'll fit those instead.

    How do I pay?

    For fitted furniture and larger jobs we usually ask for a deposit before making, with the balance on completion. Small jobs are paid when finished.

    Are you insured?

    Any carpenter working in your home should carry public liability insurance. Ask us for our insurance details and we'll send them before we start.

    How much mess is there?

    Most fitted furniture is built in a workshop, so on-site mess is limited. When we cut on site we use dust extraction, protect floors and tidy up daily.

    Can you work with other trades?

    Yes. We often work alongside electricians, plasterers and decorators. We'll agree a sequence so sockets, lighting and painting happen at the right time.
