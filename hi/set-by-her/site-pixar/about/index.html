About Set By Her

Set By Her is a tiling business based in Warrington, tiling bathrooms, showers, wet rooms, kitchens and floors for homeowners and landlords across the area. We think the most important part of tiling is the work you never see: flat, strong backgrounds, waterproofing wherever water will hit, the right adhesive for the tile and a layout planned so cuts fall where nobody notices. That's what keeps tiles looking good and stops leaks into the rooms below. We'll help you work out quantities, give honest advice on tile choice and tell you if a wall or floor needs preparing first. We protect your home while we work, keep dust down and leave every room clean. Whether it's a quick regrout, a new splashback or a full wet room, call 07552 897039 and we'll arrange a visit to measure up and talk it through.

How it works

  1. Visit and quote

    We measure, check walls and floors for flatness and strength, and send a written, itemised quote.

  2. Plan the layout

    We help with tile quantities and show you where full tiles and cuts will fall before anything is fixed.

  3. Prepare

    Old tiles come off, walls are boarded or tanked, and floors are overlaid or levelled as needed.

  4. Tile and grout

    Tiles are fixed with the correct adhesive, levelled, grouted and finished with trims and silicone.

  5. Clean and hand over

    Haze is removed, the room is cleaned and you get care advice for your tile and grout.

What we promise

  • A written, itemised quote before we start
  • Setting out agreed with you before tiles are fixed
  • Waterproofing in every area that gets direct water
  • A clean, tidy home at the end of every day
  • We'll come back to fix any defect in our work
