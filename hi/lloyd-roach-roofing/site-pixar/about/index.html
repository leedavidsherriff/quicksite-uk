About

About Lloyd Colin Roach Roofing

Lloyd Colin Roach Roofing is a local roofing firm working across Tonypandy and the surrounding area. We are small by choice, so the roofer who inspects your roof is part of the team that fixes it, and you always have a direct line to call. Our approach is simple: find the real problem, show you photos, and give you a fixed written price. If a repair will do the job, we will tell you, even when a new roof would be a bigger order for us. When a roof does need replacing, we build it to the current British Standard, with a breathable membrane, new battens and mechanically fixed ridges and verges. We handle repairs, re-roofs, flat roofs, chimneys and rooflines, keep sites tidy and treat your home with respect. Call 07811 339098 to book an inspection.

How it works

  1. Inspection

    We look at the roof and, where we can, the loft, and take photos so you can see what we see.

  2. Written quote

    You get a fixed written price with scaffold, materials and the method set out line by line, and an honest view on repair versus replacement.

  3. Access and protection

    Scaffold or a tower goes up where needed, and we protect paths, plants and conservatories below.

  4. The work

    We carry out the repair or re-roof, keeping the roof watertight at the end of every day.

  5. Photos and handover

    We clear up, show you photos of the finished work and confirm your guarantee in writing.

What we promise

  • Photos of the problem and of the finished repair
  • A fixed written price, with scaffold shown separately
  • An honest answer on repair versus replacement
  • The roof made watertight at the end of every working day
  • Public liability insurance, with the certificate shown on request
