    How it works

    From hello to handover.

    No hard sell. You already have a guide price, the survey fixes it in writing.

    1. Visit and quote

      We measure, check walls and floors for flatness and strength, and send a written, itemised quote.

    2. Plan the layout

      We help with tile quantities and show you where full tiles and cuts will fall before anything is fixed.

    3. Prepare

      Old tiles come off, walls are boarded or tanked, and floors are overlaid or levelled as needed.

    4. Tile and grout

      Tiles are fixed with the correct adhesive, levelled, grouted and finished with trims and silicone.

    5. Clean and hand over

      Haze is removed, the room is cleaned and you get care advice for your tile and grout.

    Questions

    Things people ask me.

    More on the questions page.

    Do you give free quotes?

    Yes. We visit, measure, check the walls and floors and send a written quote with preparation, tiling and materials itemised. Small jobs can often be quoted from photos.

    Do you supply the tiles?

    Most customers choose their own tiles. We'll advise on quantities and suitability, and supply adhesive, grout, trims and boards to suit the tile.

    How do I pay?

    For larger jobs we may ask for a deposit for materials, with the balance on completion. Small jobs are paid on completion. You'll get an itemised invoice.

    Are you insured?

    Any tiler working in your home should carry public liability insurance. Ask us for our insurance details and we'll send them before we start.

    Can you work with my plumber and electrician?

    Yes. Tiling usually goes after first-fix plumbing and electrics, and before fitting sanitaryware and fronts. We'll agree a sequence with your other trades.

    How much mess is there?

    Removing old tiles is dusty and noisy. We sheet floors, seal doorways, use dust extraction when cutting, and clean up at the end of each day.
