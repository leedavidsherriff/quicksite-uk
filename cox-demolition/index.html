How a job runs

Four steps. No surprises.

1. Tony looks

Send photos on WhatsApp or have him round. He checks access, what it's built from and where the muck goes.

2. Checks done

Asbestos survey arranged if the building needs one. Services cut off. Council notice sorted if the size of the job calls for it.

3. Down it comes

Machine in, structure down, the house next door and your neighbour's fence left exactly as they were.

4. Cleared & level

Rubble loaded and away, ground left level and swept, ready for whatever goes there next.

Questions

What people ask first.

How much does it cost to knock down a garage in Bridgend?

As a guide, a concrete sectional garage is £950 to £1,090 and a single brick garage with the base broken out is £1,850 to £2,120, taken down and taken away. Every job is priced properly after a visit.

Do you take the rubble away?

Yes. Muck away is by 8-wheeler, about £290 to £330 a load as a guide, and less per load when three or more go the same day. Knock-down prices already include taking the building away.

Can I hire the digger with a driver?

Yes. Excavator hire comes with an operator, by the day. As a guide a mini digger and operator is £290 to £330 a day and a 13-tonne machine and operator is £520 to £595 a day, plus delivery.

Do you deal with asbestos?

We don't remove asbestos ourselves. For anything built before 2000 we arrange a licensed asbestos survey before work starts, and anything found is removed by a licensed contractor before the machine goes in.

Do I need permission to knock a building down?

Small outbuildings usually don't. Larger buildings normally need a demolition notice to the council before work starts, and listed buildings or conservation areas need consent. Tony will tell you what applies to your job when he comes to look.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Pyle and work across Bridgend county, Porthcawl, Maesteg, Port Talbot, Cardiff and the rest of South Wales.
