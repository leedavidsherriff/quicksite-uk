Porthcawl Chiropractic · Guide

Seeing a chiropractor near Bridgend — prices and your first appointment

By Porthcawl Chiropractic Clinic · 2026-10-07

Short answer: at Porthcawl Chiropractic, Olwen Griffiths' clinic at Portway Wellness Centre, the first appointment is £72 for 45 minutes and includes treatment. Follow-up treatments are £40.

Olwen also arranges X-ray, MRI and DEXA scans where needed — ask about these when you call.

Who you'll see

Olwen Griffiths is a GCC-registered chiropractor who has looked after patients in Porthcawl since 2008. The clinic is female-run, and Olwen treats in English or Welsh.

What she treats

Back pain, neck pain, joint problems and posture, plus sports injuries and pregnancy-related care.

Health insurance

AXA PPP, Pru Health, Welsh Hospitals and Cigna are accepted.

Questions

How do I book?

Ring Olwen on 01656 331551 — she prefers to be contacted personally by phone, and doesn't use email for patient correspondence.

How far is it from Bridgend?

About 15–20 minutes' drive — the clinic is at Portway Wellness Centre, 1 The Portway, Porthcawl CF36 3XB, with parking.

Do I need a GP referral?

No referral is needed to book with a chiropractor; just ring to make an appointment.
