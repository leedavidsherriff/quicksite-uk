Invigorate · Guide

Sauna and cold plunge in Porthcawl — prices and what to expect

By Invigorate · 2026-10-07

Short answer: Invigorate, at Portway Wellness Centre on The Portway, runs a stone sauna and cold plunge you can book by the session. A standard 50-minute session is £20, and there are packs and monthly memberships if you go regularly.

These are Invigorate's published prices; check the booking page for the current list and any offers.

What a session is like

You book a 50-minute slot. Inside there's the stone sauna, the cold plunge, showers and changing, plus massage guns to use afterwards. Most people move between the heat and the cold at their own pace — there's no set routine you have to follow.

What to bring

Swimwear, a towel and a bottle of water. Sessions run in hourly slots through the day, so pick a time that suits you on the booking page.

Is it right for me?

Hot and cold sessions aren't for everyone. If you're pregnant, have a heart condition or high blood pressure, or have any other health condition, check with your GP before you book.

Questions

How do I book?

Online through Invigorate's booking page — casual sessions, packs, memberships and gift cards are all there. You can also call the centre on 01656 856120.

Do I have to join?

No. You can book a single session, buy a pack of 5 or 10, or join a monthly club if you'll go every week.

Where is it?

Inside Portway Wellness Centre, 1 The Portway, Porthcawl CF36 3XB — the old surgery building, with parking.
