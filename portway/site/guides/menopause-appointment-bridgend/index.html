Môr Health · Guide

Private menopause appointments near Bridgend

By Môr Health · 2026-10-07

Short answer: Môr Health in Porthcawl offers a dedicated menopause and women's health appointment with Dr Kate Evans. The first appointment is 45 minutes and costs £225; follow-ups are 30 minutes at £140.

Who you'd see

Dr Kate Evans is a GP with a special interest in women's health. She's a member of the British Menopause Society and holds the DRCOG and DFSRH qualifications. Môr Health is registered with Healthcare Inspectorate Wales.

Prices

AppointmentLengthPrice
First appointment45 minutes£225
Follow-up30 minutes£140

These are Môr Health's published prices. Blood tests are available from £40 plus lab fees if they're needed.

Why people book a longer appointment

Menopause symptoms can be wide-ranging, and many people want time to talk them through properly. A 45-minute first appointment gives room for that.

You can also see your NHS GP

Menopause care is available through the NHS too, and your own GP surgery is always an option. Booking privately is a choice about time and timing, not a requirement.

Questions

How do I book?

Online through Môr Health's booking page, or call 01656 479500.

How far is it from Bridgend?

Môr Health is in Porthcawl, at Portway Wellness Centre, 1 The Portway — about 15–20 minutes' drive from Bridgend town, with parking at the building.

Will I see the same doctor at follow-ups?

Môr Health's approach is continuity: longer appointments with the same doctor each time.
