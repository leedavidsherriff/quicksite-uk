Using an insurance broker in Porthcawl — what a broker does, and how to get started

By Portway Financial · 2026-10-07

Short answer: Portway Financial, based at Portway Wellness Centre on The Portway, is an insurance broker for people and businesses in Porthcawl and across Bridgend county. A broker arranges cover with insurers on your behalf and explains what a policy does and doesn't include.

What a broker does

A comparison website shows you prices. A broker talks you through what you need covered, arranges the policy with an insurer, and is there to help if you need to make a change or a claim.

What Portway Financial can help with

ForCover
You and your familyPersonal insurance
Your businessBusiness and commercial insurance, including employers' liability

If you employ staff, employers' liability insurance is usually a legal requirement — most employers in Great Britain must have it.

What to have ready

  • What you want to insure, and roughly what it's worth
  • Your current policy and renewal date, if you have one
  • For a business: what you do, how many people you employ, and your turnover
  • Any claims in the last five years
