About the centre

One building. Every aspect of wellness.

Portway Wellness Centre is Porthcawl's dedicated home for health, wellness and beauty professionals. Purpose-designed and welcoming, the building brings specialist businesses together in one place.

Whether you're coming for a sauna session, a chiropractic adjustment, physiotherapy, an aesthetic treatment, a private GP consultation, financial advice or a tan, it's all here under one roof.

A place where professionals thrive and clients receive the very best care — all in the heart of Porthcawl.
