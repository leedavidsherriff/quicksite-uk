About Porthcawl Physiotherapy

Porthcawl Physiotherapy & Sports Injury Clinic: assessment and treatment for sports injuries, rehabilitation, chronic pain and mobility — face to face or remote — with personalised treatment plans.

Run by chartered physiotherapist Helen C. Morgan (HCPC registered) since 1996. Open Mon–Thu 8:30am–5:30pm, Fri 8:30am–4:30pm.

  • Sports injuries
  • Massage & manipulation
  • Electrotherapy
  • Pilates & exercise
  • Custom foot orthotics

Questions

What do you treat?

Sports injuries, rehabilitation after surgery, chronic pain and mobility problems, using massage, manipulation, electrotherapy, Pilates-based exercise and custom foot orthotics.

Can I have a remote appointment?

Yes. Assessment and treatment are available face to face or remotely.

Who will I see?

Helen C. Morgan, a chartered physiotherapist registered with the HCPC, who has run the clinic since 1996.
