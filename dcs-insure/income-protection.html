What this cover is for

A monthly benefit while you can't play

Income protection pays a monthly benefit while you are unable to work through illness or injury, on terms set by the insurer. For a footballer or rugby player, "unable to work" usually means unable to train or play — the policy needs to reflect that, which is exactly the kind of detail a standard high-street policy often doesn't.

Whether income protection is suitable for you, and at what level, is a conversation about your contract, your club and how you're actually paid. We introduce and arrange; we don't advise on this website and nothing here is a quotation.

  • Cover shaped around sporting income — match fees, contract pay, sponsorship — not a generic salary.
  • Options for semi-professional and professional players, and for self-employed tradespeople paid by the day.
  • Introduced through insurers on our panel — terms, exclusions and eligibility are theirs, not ours.
