Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. If the rubbish is outside or Tony can get to it, he can clear it while you're out.

Where does it all go?

To licensed waste and recycling sites, and as much as possible is recycled.

Can the skip go on the road?

Yes, with a council permit. Tony can sort that for you, it's added to the price.

What can't go in?

Asbestos, gas bottles, paint, oils and chemicals. Ask Tony if you're not sure.

Which areas?

Pyle, Porthcawl, Bridgend, Maesteg, Port Talbot, Cardiff and the rest of South Wales.
