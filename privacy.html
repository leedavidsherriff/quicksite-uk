Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

In short

You tell us your trade and your number, we build you a website preview and send it to you. We use your details for that and nothing else. Ask us to delete them and we will.

Who we are

This site and the QuickSite website service are run by Quick Site Digital Limited. We are the “data controller” for the details you send us, which means we decide how they are used and we are responsible for looking after them.

Where your details come from

Usually from a form you fill in on one of our Facebook or Instagram adverts, asking us to build you a website preview. Sometimes you message or ring us instead, or send us details after we have spoken.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your business name, your trade and the town you work in.
  • Whether you already have a website, and how you would like us to get back to you.
  • Anything you send us afterwards for your site — your prices, your photos, the hours you work.

We do not ask for payment card details, and we never ask for them by message or over the phone.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your details to build your website preview, to send it to you, and to talk to you about it. If you decide to keep the site, we use them to run it and to bill you.

  • Consent — you gave us your details and asked for a preview. You can withdraw that at any time by emailing us, and we will stop contacting you.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping a record of who we have spoken to and what we have built, so we do not pester people twice and can answer questions later.
  • Contract — if you become a customer, to provide the service you are paying for.

Who we share it with

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) — the advert form runs on their platform, so they hold a copy of what you submit. Their own privacy policy covers that.
  • Our technology suppliers — the companies that host our systems and your website, who process the data on our instructions.

That is all. We do not sell your details, we do not pass them to other trades, and we do not use them to advertise anything unrelated to your website.

How long we keep it

If you do not go ahead, we delete your details twelve months after we last hear from you. If you become a customer, we keep them while we run your site, and for six years afterwards because tax rules require us to keep billing records.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your details, correct them, delete them, or stop using them. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email lee@quick-site.co.uk and we will deal with it within one month. There is no charge.

Cookies and measurement

We do not use cookies on this site at all — not our own, and none from anyone else. There is more detail on the cookies page. We do count page views so we know which pages get read: that record is the page name, the domain of the site you arrived from, and the date. No cookie, no IP address, no name, and nothing that identifies you or your device. The demo sites we build carry the same simple counter.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, please tell us first at lee@quick-site.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your details we will update this page, and the date at the top will tell you when it last changed.
