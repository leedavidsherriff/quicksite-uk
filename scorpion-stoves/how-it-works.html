How it works

Four steps to a lit fire.

A free look at your room, a price in writing, two days of fitting and the first fire lit with you. That’s it.

Book a free surveyOr order online

GarethNo salesman, no pushy follow-ups. Just me, a tape measure and a cuppa.

Free survey

Gareth comes out and checks your chimney, flue and hearth clearances, and talks through the six fireplaces in your actual room. No obligation.

Your price in writing

The guide prices on this site are for a typical room. After the survey you get one confirmed price, in writing, before any work starts. If anything about your chimney changes the job, you hear it then, not halfway through.

Fitted in two days

Dust sheets down first thing on day one. Day two is the finish: hearth, mantel, flue checks. The Showpiece stonework and the Media Wall can run to a third day.

First burn, done with you

Gareth lights the first fire with you, shows you the air controls and what to burn, and fits a CO alarm with every stove. He’ll go through the sign-off paperwork for your install on the survey.
