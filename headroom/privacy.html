Privacy notice

How we collect, use and look after your personal data.

Who we are

Just Business Funding is a trading name of Portway Financial Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14917868), whose registered office is 1 The Portway, Porthcawl, CF36 3XB. Portway Financial Limited is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice.

Portway Financial Limited is an Appointed Representative of Connect IFA Limited, which is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. Where your data is passed to Connect IFA Limited or to a lender, that organisation will handle it under its own privacy policy.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, your business name, your mobile number and your email address.
  • The answers you give us about your business and what you need: what the money is for, roughly how much, how soon, how the business is set up, how long it has traded, its monthly turnover, how customers pay you, whether a director or owner owns a home, and the best time to call.
  • If you go on to apply for finance, the information a lender needs to consider it, such as bank statements, accounts and proof of identity.
  • Records of our correspondence with you, including calls, messages and emails.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your personal data to respond to your enquiry, to explain the kinds of finance that may suit your business, to introduce you to lenders you have agreed to, and to meet our regulatory and legal obligations. Our lawful bases are: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract; our legitimate interests in operating and administering our business; your consent, where you have given it, including to be contacted about your enquiry; and compliance with a legal obligation.

If you are a sole trader or in a partnership, the information about your business is also information about you, and we treat it as personal data.

Credit searches

We do not run a credit search when you answer our questions or send us your enquiry. If you choose to apply to a lender, that lender may search your records, and the records of your business, with one or more credit reference agencies. We will tell you before we pass your details to any lender, so you can decide whether to go ahead.

Who we share it with

We only share your details with a lender or finance provider after you have agreed to it. We may also share your personal data with our principal firm, Connect IFA Limited, and with the software and administration suppliers we use to run our business, including the messaging and email services your enquiry arrives through. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep your personal data for as long as is necessary for the purposes described above and to meet our regulatory obligations. Where finance is arranged through us, this is generally a minimum of six years after our relationship ends, and longer where the lender or the regulator requires it. If your enquiry does not go ahead, we delete your details within 12 months unless you ask us to stay in touch.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, and, in certain circumstances, to have your data erased or its use restricted, to object to our processing of it, and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, including by telling us you no longer want us to contact you.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@portwaygroup.com, call 01656 856120, or write to Just Business Funding, 1 The Portway, Porthcawl, CF36 3XB.

Cookies and measurement

This website sets no cookies of its own and does not track you across other websites. We do count how many times each page is opened and how many times each answer button is tapped, so we know which questions are clear and which adverts are useful. Each count records only the page or button, the day, and the website you arrived from. It does not record your IP address, your device, your answers alongside your details, or anything that could identify you, and it is not shared with any advertising network. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal, we don’t count anything at all.

We may use a Meta (Facebook) measurement cookie to see whether our adverts lead to enquiries. We only load it if you agree when the site asks, and the site works the same if you say no. You can change your mind by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, or by calling 0303 123 1113.

Portway Financial Limited is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office under reference ZB625186.
