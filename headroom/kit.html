Four Facebook and Instagram ads, each with its own link into Penny’s questions. Every tap is counted anonymously, so we can see which ad brings leads and where people give up. Each finished enquiry lands on WhatsApp or email, ready for a call.

The four ads

Feed format, 1080×1350. The images have no text in them, so the words live in the ad itself. These links point at the review copy for now, and get swapped for the real address at launch.

Replying to a lead

Leads go cold in minutes. Each enquiry arrives with the person’s name, need, amount and best time to call, so these fill in quickly. Send from the business WhatsApp, never a personal number.

On the first call

  1. Confirm what they told Penny: need, amount, how soon.
  2. Check how they’re set up. A sole trader or partnership borrowing £25k or less is regulated credit, and the lead is flagged when it is.
  3. Ask about recent bank statements, accounts, and any existing finance.
  4. Explain the routes plainly, including rates, fees and total repayable before anything is agreed.
  5. Say how we’re paid (lender commission) before introducing a lender.
  6. Get their go-ahead before sharing details with any lender.

Reading the funnel

  • dash.html shows opens, drop-off at each question, leads per ad and which finance type comes up most. It works once the site is on its real address.
  • Give each ad about £10 a day for a week, then move budget to whichever brings the most leads per open.
  • If most people stop at one question, that question is the problem. Reword it or drop it.
  • The counter stores no names or numbers, only which button was tapped.
