Replying to a lead

Leads go cold in minutes. Each enquiry arrives with the person’s name, need, amount and best time to call, so these fill in quickly. Send from the business WhatsApp, never a personal number.

On the first call

  1. Confirm what they told Penny: need, amount, how soon.
  2. Check how they’re set up. A sole trader or partnership borrowing £25k or less is regulated credit, and the lead is flagged when it is.
  3. Ask about recent bank statements, accounts, and any existing finance.
  4. Explain the routes plainly, including rates, fees and total repayable before anything is agreed.
  5. Say how we’re paid (lender commission) before introducing a lender.
  6. Get their go-ahead before sharing details with any lender.

Reading the funnel

  • dash.html shows opens, drop-off at each question, leads per ad and which finance type comes up most. It works once the site is on its real address.
  • Give each ad about £10 a day for a week, then move budget to whichever brings the most leads per open.
  • If most people stop at one question, that question is the problem. Reword it or drop it.
  • The counter stores no names or numbers, only which button was tapped.

Google Business Profile

Free, and it puts Talk to Penny on Google Maps and in “business loan near me” searches. Set up at business.google.com with Dale’s or Portway’s Google account. I can’t create accounts, and Google checks the business by postcard or video call.

Check first: Google only lists businesses that meet customers in person. The pack below lists the Portway office for meetings by appointment. That’s only allowed if Dale will actually meet clients there, so confirm it before you set up. For the video check, have something with the Talk to Penny name on it at the office, such as a printed sign or letterhead.

Setting it up

  1. Go to business.google.com and choose Add business.
  2. Name: Talk to Penny. Category: Loan agency.
  3. Add the office address and choose to show it, since clients can visit by appointment.
  4. Add the service areas, phone and website below.
  5. Verify by postcard or video. Video: show the Talk to Penny sign, the office and the street.
  6. Once verified, paste in the description, services, hours and photos, then publish the first post.
  7. Ask real clients for reviews after a deal. Never pay for them or offer anything in return.
