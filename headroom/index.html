Straight talking

What we are.
What we’re not.

We will

  • Tell you if it’s a no. If nothing fits yet, we’ll say so and tell you what would change it.
  • Explain the real cost. Rates, fees and what you’d repay, before you commit to anything.
  • Be free to you. We’re paid by the lender if you go ahead, and we’ll tell you how.

We won’t

  • Lend you money. We’re a broker, not a lender.
  • Promise approval. Every lender makes its own decision, subject to status.
  • Sell your details. Shared only with the lenders you agree to.
Questions

The things people ask.

Will checking hurt my credit score?

No. Penny’s questions don’t involve a credit search. If you later choose to apply to a lender, they may run a search — and we’ll tell you before that happens.

What does it cost me?

Nothing to use Talk to Penny. If you take out finance, the lender pays us a commission. We’ll tell you about it before you go ahead.

I’ve been turned down by my bank. Is there any point?

Often, yes. Banks are one lender among many, and some types of finance — invoice finance, asset finance, cash advances — look at different things. We’ll be honest if the answer is still no.

Do I have to go ahead?

No. You can stop at any point, including after we’ve talked the options through.

I’ve only just started trading.

Options are thinner for very new businesses, but not zero. Tell us anyway and we’ll say what’s realistic.
