Finance for builders and trades

Trades businesses are often busy and short of cash at the same time: materials bought up front, main contractors paying in 60 days, and retentions held back for months.

Match the money to the problem

  • Waiting on main contractors? Invoice finance releases most of an invoice’s value now, instead of in 60 days.
  • Need a van, digger or kit? Asset finance spreads the cost, with the item as security.
  • Taking on a bigger contract? A business loan can fund materials and labour up front.
  • Doing up your own property? Bridging or development finance might fit.

What lenders like to see

Steady turnover in your bank statements, contracts or repeat customers, and up-to-date accounts if you’re a limited company. If you’re a sole trader, recent tax returns help.

Retentions and applications for payment

Money tied up in retentions is hard to borrow against directly. Invoice finance providers who understand construction can sometimes work with applications for payment. Tell us how you invoice and we’ll look at what fits.

Questions people ask

I’m a subcontractor. Does invoice finance work for me?

It can, especially if you invoice main contractors on terms. Some providers specialise in construction.

Can I finance tools and small kit?

Yes. Asset finance and business credit cards both work for smaller purchases.
