Gates hung to swing true for years

Side gates and driveway gates that match the fence and close without a shove.

A gate is the one part of the fence you use every day, and a badly hung one tells on itself within weeks: it drops, it catches on the path, the latch stops meeting the keep and you end up lifting it to close it. Nearly every sagging gate comes from the post, not the gate. We hang side gates and driveway gates on posts sized for the weight, set deep in concrete, with hinges and latches rated for outdoor use, and we make or supply the gate to match the fence, so the boundary reads as one line.

Side gates

A side or pedestrian gate is usually about 0.9 to 1 metre wide and 1.8 metres high to match a back-garden fence. We make it in the same style as the fence, closeboard, slatted, hit and miss or a composite-framed gate, with a diagonal brace running up from the hinge side so it cannot drop at the latch end. It hangs on heavy tee or strap hinges, with a latch or a lockable bolt on the garden side. If security matters, we can fit a gate lock or a padbolt and make sure the hinges cannot be lifted off from outside.

Driveway gates

Double driveway gates, typically covering an opening of about 3 to 3.6 metres, are heavy and they catch the wind, so they go on large-section posts, often 150 mm, set deeper than fence posts. We add a drop bolt into the ground to hold one leaf, a gate stop, and a latch between the leaves. In Scotland gates next to a road or pavement should open inwards onto your property, not out over the footway, and we set them up that way. We can build them solid for privacy or slatted to let the wind through.

New posts or reuse the old ones

If you are replacing just the gate, we check the posts first. A post that has started to rot at ground level or lean will pull a new gate out of line within a season, and it is cheaper to replace it now than to rehang the gate later. Where the posts are sound, we can hang a new gate on them and adjust the hinges so it swings level. Your written price will say which we are doing and why.

Keeping a gate swinging

A drop of oil on the hinges and latch once a year, a check that the brace is still tight, and keeping the ground beneath the gate clear of soil and moss is all most gates need. Timber gates want the same stain or treatment as the fence around them. If a gate starts to catch, it is usually a hinge screw working loose; tighten it early and it will not go further.

How long it takes

A side gate is usually half a day; driveway gates about a day, plus time for the post concrete to set.

Questions

Garden gates: what people ask.

How much does a new garden gate cost?

As a guide, a matching timber side gate hung on sound posts runs about £380 to £430, or about £480 to £545 with two new posts. Double timber driveway gates with new posts are around £1,150 to £1,300.

Why does my gate keep dropping?

Usually either the hinge-side post is loose or rotting at ground level, or the gate has no diagonal brace, or the brace runs the wrong way. The brace should run up from the bottom hinge towards the latch side so it pushes the gate up.

Which way should a driveway gate open?

Inwards, onto your own ground. A gate beside a road or pavement should not open outwards over the footway, and inward-opening gates are also easier to secure.
