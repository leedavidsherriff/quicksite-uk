The tour

Room by room,
the way Guy builds them.

No plans. No artist's impressions. Self-taught, and building by eye on site. Here's where the money goes in a Guy Phoenix Superhome, and why.

01 · Entrance hall

The first ten seconds

“Entrance halls are the most important thing of any Superhome. This is number one thing you see when you walk into the house.”

Staircase, doors, chandelier. Spend the extra here before anywhere else.

02 · The pool

Bigger than your house

“The average size house is about 1,100 square foot in the UK. Our swimming pool on its own is bigger than the average size house.”

Underground pools, waterfalls, jacuzzis, glass floors looking down from the hall. That's a Superhome.

03 · The staircase

Hermitage's helix

“Stand here, look at the chandelier, look at the stairs.”

Seven suites on two and a half acres in Edwalton, with a champagne room, a cinema, a panic room and a pool you can see through the glass floor.

04 · The layout

Before a single tile

“The most crucial thing is getting the layout right in the house.”

Where the dishwasher goes, where you sit, where the fridges live. Finishes come later. Superhome or terrace, the same steps apply.
