Cookies

Last updated 16 September 2026

The short version

This website doesn't use cookies. There's no banner to click because there's nothing to consent to.

What a cookie is, and why we don't set one

A cookie is a small file a website stores on your device, usually so it can recognise you again later. Most business websites use them to run analytics or to follow you around the internet with adverts. We don't do either, so this site sets no cookies — none of our own, and none from anyone else.

The same goes for the other ways a site can store things on your device: we don't use local storage, session storage or browser fingerprinting.

So how do you count visitors?

We count page views, and only page views. When a page loads, it sends us two things: the name of the page you're on, and the domain name of the site that sent you here (for example facebook.com, or nothing at all if you typed the address in). That's it.

What we deliberately do not collect: your IP address, your name, your device, an ID that could be matched to you later, or anything about where you go next. Two visits from the same person are indistinguishable from two different people. It tells us whether the roofing page gets read more than the tiling page, and nothing else.

Other people's content on this page

  • The films. The videos are served from Amazon CloudFront, a content delivery network. It delivers the video file and sets no cookies.
  • Links that leave this site. The app, the example sites and the Companies House register open in a new tab and are governed by their own privacy and cookie policies, not this one.

Do Not Track

There's nothing here to switch off. Browser privacy settings, tracker blockers and Do Not Track headers will all find nothing to block on this site, which is the intended result.

If this ever changes

If we add anything that needs a cookie — an advertising pixel, a third-party analytics tool, an embedded player that tracks you — we will put a proper consent banner in front of it and ask you first, and this page will be updated before it goes live. We will not quietly start tracking you.

Questions

How we handle the details you actually send us — your name, your trade, your town — is covered in the privacy policy. Anything else, email lee@quick-site.co.uk and a person will reply.
