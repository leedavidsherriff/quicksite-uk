About us

A small British company that builds websites for trades.

Quick Site Digital Limited was set up to fix one thing: tradespeople doing excellent work behind websites that make them look like nobody. We build the site, we run it, and we charge one monthly price for it.

How it works

Four steps, and only one of them is yours.

Tell us your trade and town

That's genuinely all we need to start. A message, an email, or a two-minute brief.

We build it

Your words, your trade's film, your prices. A real site on a real URL — usually within 24 hours.

You look at it

Open it on your phone. Send it to your wife, your mate, your best customer. Change anything you don't like.

£30 a month if you keep it

Hosting, changes and the film included. If you don't want it, you've paid nothing and you owe nothing.

What we won't do

Being straight about the limits is the fastest way to be believed about everything else.

We won't invent reviews

If there are no client quotes on this site yet, it's because we haven't got real ones to show. We'd rather have a gap than a lie.

We won't promise you Google

A good site helps. Anyone guaranteeing you the top of page one for a fixed monthly fee is selling you something they can't deliver.

We won't tie you in first

You see the finished site before money is mentioned. That's the whole model — it only works if the site is actually good.

Talk to a person

No call centre and no chatbot. Email goes to Lee, who will either build you a site or tell you straight that we're not the right fit.
