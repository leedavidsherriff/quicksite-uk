Decorators: the questions we get asked

Will it reassure people about mess?

Directly. Customers fear mess more than price, so dust sheets, prep and the tidy-up are promised in writing on the page rather than left to the first phone call.

Can I show interior and exterior separately?

Yes — they are different jobs bought by different customers at different times of year, and the site prices and presents them separately.

How much does a decorating website cost?

£30 a month, and nothing at all until you have seen it. There is no setup fee, no design deposit and no separate hosting bill. That price covers the site, the hosting, your trade film and changes whenever your work changes.

How long before my site is live?

Usually within 24 hours of you telling us your trade and your town. You get a link to a real, working site on a real URL — not a mock-up in an email — and you decide after you have opened it on your own phone.

Do I have to pay anything up front?

No. We build it first and send you the link. If you do not like it you have paid nothing and you owe nothing. That only works if the site is genuinely good, which is rather the point.

I already get work from Facebook. Why do I need a website?

Because a Facebook page cannot be found on Google when someone searches “decorating near me” at nine at night, it cannot show your prices, and you do not own it. A website does all three, and it keeps working while you are on the tools.

What if I want to change something later?

Tell us and it is done — new price, new photo, new service. Changes are included in the monthly price. Or make the change yourself from the dashboard app on your phone, or ask the AI chatbot.

Am I tied into a contract?

No. It is £30 a month for as long as you want it. We would rather keep you because the site earns its keep than because you are locked in.
