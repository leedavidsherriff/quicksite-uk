Rules it follows by itself

9am to 8pm only

Nothing goes out at night except a confirmation or cancellation someone has just made.

3 offers a month, tops

Never two offer texts within 4 days. Every one says how to stop.

Consent first

Offers only go to people who ticked the box when booking (UK PECR rules).

Fair reviews

Everyone gets the same ask, no "were you happy?" filter and no freebies. Google and the DMCC Act ban both.

Your part

  1. Answer replies in the GHL app. Anyone who texts back lands in Conversations, from 07888 865035.
  2. Tick "Arrived" in Admin. It's what lets the "sorry we missed you" text work, and stops no-shows getting a review ask.
  3. Press "Text the regulars" on a quiet session in Admin, Chasing & reviews.
  4. Kit orders: Admin, Merch. Press Ready (it texts them), then Collected.
