Worried-homeowner questions

The stuff everyone asks

How long will it take?

Rough rule: a boiler swap is a day, a bathroom about a week, a patio 3 to 5 days, and a single-storey extension 10 to 14 weeks. You’ll get a start date and a finish date in writing, and if anything changes you’ll hear it from Jon first.

How much mess will there be?

Some. It’s building work. But dust sheets go down before tools come out, doorways get sealed off on dusty jobs, and we sweep up at the end of every day, not just the last one. Customers keep mentioning the tidiness, so we must be doing something right.

Will I need to move out?

Almost never. For a bathroom you’ll be without it for about a week; if it’s your only toilet, tell us and we’ll plan round it. For an extension, the kitchen’s out for a couple of weeks around the knock-through. Boilers: no hot water for a day.

What if you find something nasty?

Then you see it before it’s fixed. Rotten joists, dodgy old wiring, a leak nobody knew about: Jon shows you, explains it, and gives you the price. Nothing extra goes on the bill without you saying yes.

Are your prices quotes?

The prices on this site are honest guides for typical Cardiff jobs, so you know roughly where you stand. Your actual price is fixed in writing after Jon’s had a look, before any work starts.

Do I need planning permission?

Lots of single-storey rear extensions and most garden walls don’t need full planning permission, but extensions always need building regulations sign-off. It depends on your house, so ask Jon on the first visit and he’ll tell you straight.
