How we work

Plenty of our work comes from people who’ve had us before, or whose daughter or neighbour has. That only happens if you do the basics properly, every single time.

So: I come and look before I give you a price. The price is in writing. If we find a problem nobody could have seen, like rotten joists under a bathroom floor, I show you before we fix it, and you get the price first. Nothing sneaks onto the invoice.

We turn up when we say. We put dust sheets down. We sweep up every evening. And when we’re done, the skip goes when we do.
